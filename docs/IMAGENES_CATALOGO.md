## Prompt común
Use case: product-mockup. One photorealistic ecommerce catalog photograph of men's [subject]. One product design only. Clothing uses invisible mannequin, no person, no hanger. Entire product centered with generous 15% white margins, pure white #ffffff seamless background, soft studio lighting, realistic detailed materials, subtle contact shadow only, no text, no logos, no watermark. Square image. Fictional demo store product.
